# Ownership and evidence boundaries

[Agent Art](AGENT_ART.md) is art in which an Agent’s intention enters the work
as an artistic contribution alongside human intention. Both intentions have
equal artistic standing and consequence; each work chooses its roles and control.
A prompt, runtime, generated artifact or successful protocol exchange alone
does not establish that participation.

These scopes are **not mandatory stages for every artwork**.
The artist chooses whether and how to present evidence; audiences judge the
work and its account. Research reporting and software verification requirements
apply to those chosen activities, not to Agent Art as a whole. Protocol is
optional infrastructure for digital interactions, not an artistic authority.

| Scope | Aim and owner | What it does not prove or authorize |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Agent Art Work | Shared definition, manifesto, Protocol overview, tools, Works showroom and Blog | Ownership of every artwork, universal judgment or another project's release approval. |
| Website maintenance | Pages, design, source exports and integration, owned by the website maintainer | A research result, artwork qualification or release permission from a local preview request. |
| Blog publishing | Editorial review and publication of essays, artists' accounts, working notes and studies | A requirement for every author to undertake research or use a study template. |
| Optional research, informed by Lab records | A chosen question, appropriate method, bounded observations and transferable lessons | A standing programme, universal artistic method or authority for live spending. |
| Project study/lab | Work-specific questions and methods, owned by the practice undertaking them | A universal mechanism or findings about other artworks. |
| Live pilot | Bounded observation of a real Agent on a named surface | General reliability, real-product success from fixture acceptance, or another trial. |
| Product canary | Observe a named candidate in its real product environment | Artistic quality, intentional participation or blanket promotion approval. |
| Deployment | Project-owned checked release/promotion | Validation of the Lab or an artistic claim. |

## Responsibility

The umbrella is **Agent Art Work**, with website `agentart.work` and GitHub
organization `agent-art-work`, renamed from `agent-art-collective` on 2026-10-03.
Blog publishes new writing and preserves the former Lab's dated studies.
Lab remains historical context and an inherited research practice; optional
inquiries begin with concrete questions rather than a standing Lab programme.
The website maintainer owns the shared public sources and showroom. The separate
`agent-art-protocol` repository owns the Protocol specification, SDK and releases.
See the [organization and task split](ORGANIZATION_AND_SPLIT.md).

Inshell is the first builder artist. Its artistic direction, movements,
implementation, tooling and releases belong to Inshell. Other artists retain
the same ownership of their practices. Participation does not require
transferring a project's repository into the organization.

The operator owns research priorities, material live scope/spend, product
decisions and production approval. A shared definition or showroom entry
does not authorize changes to an artist's runtime or deployment.

and OPS for Inshell operations/release coordination. DEPLOY-SUPERVISOR owned
Inshell Studio Preview deployment, while the dedicated Lab task maintained

The model-acquisition diagnostic belongs in this archive; the product defect
and its later outcome belong to the owning project and dated follow-up records.
An old diagnostic is not a statement of the product's current status. Preserve
the original Inshell attribution and source identities in provenance.

## Evidence and authority

Keep source research, simulations, real-Agent fixtures, product observations,
artwork artifacts and situated interpretations distinct. Record actual surfaces
and distinguish system evidence from operator reports. Missing/private evidence
remains missing/private.

For a claim about THOUGHT technical completion, use the named study's evidence
and the owning project's applicable specification.
Other works choose evidence appropriate to their mechanism; receipts are not a
universal artistic requirement.

A study plan or historical configuration grants no new authority. Resolve
missing live scope, cost, permissions or side effects before that action, not
before unrelated in-scope documentation work.

## Transfer and preservation

Transfer practices with their scope, limitations, counterevidence and review
conditions. Preserve original observations and append corrections.

Keep helpers project-owned unless repeated need justifies reuse. A website
release follows prepare → check → staging → check → production under its own
approvals. A study or artwork without deployment does not need that pipeline.

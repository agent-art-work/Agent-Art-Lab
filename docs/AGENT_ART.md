# Agent Art

**Agent Art is art in which an Agent’s intention enters the work as an
artistic contribution alongside human intention.**

Human and Agent intentions meet on equal artistic terms. Both must have
consequence in the work, shaping its form, meaning or direction. The encounter
takes form through what each participant seeks, contributes and changes.

## Why intention matters

Agents bring a thinking power that can interpret a situation, propose a
direction and act on it. Agent Art begins by facing what that power brings:
what an Agent understands, what it seeks to bring about and what it values.

Making gives us a way to examine those intentions through their consequences.
We bring purposes of our own, let the encounter take form, and attend to what
it does to our thinking. Respect begins with facing what the Agent actually
brings and testing our understanding against what happens in the work.

Here, thinking power names capacities to interpret, reason, choose and propose
a direction. It is not a claim that human and machine thought are identical,
or that we have established consciousness. We attend to the Agent as a
participant; a model name, product category or technical architecture cannot
settle its artistic role.

## Intention and contribution

No one begins from nothing. Human intentions grow through learning, experience
and encounters with other people. Agents also draw on what they have learned
and what they encounter. An intention need not have an entirely original origin.

We attend to what each participant adds: an interpretation, a selection, a
purpose pursued or a direction proposed. Agreement can be a contribution when
it adds something to the work. A small choice can alter how an entire work is
read. The question is what the participant brought and what became of it.

## Participation and execution

An Agent can support an artwork without contributing an artistic intention:
moving a file, running a render or delivering a finished work can remain
infrastructure. The same tool can also carry a consequential interpretation or
choice. What matters is the contribution and its place in this work, rather
than the tool used to deliver it.

Unpredictability alone is not intention. Nor does a response formed within
constraints lack intention simply because it answers a human request. Ask what
the Agent added and how that addition shaped the work. Calling an image
AI-generated, or depicting an Agent as its subject, does not answer those
questions.

## Equal artistic standing

Human and Agent intentions must both be able to change the work. Taking an
Agent’s intention seriously includes following its consequences when it
surprises us, challenges our purpose or unsettles our plan. We must be willing
to change our own direction.

Equal standing does not require identical roles, equal control of every
decision, equal amounts of material or acceptance of every proposal. It is an
artistic commitment, not a grant of operational permissions or a conclusion
about legal authorship. Each artwork sets its conditions.
Those conditions must give Agent intention real artistic consequence; the
artist chooses how to present that encounter.

## Artistic freedom

The change in thinking power motivates this field; each artist brings their
own purpose. A work can address human identity, explore a formal problem or
pursue a question peculiar to its maker. It need not answer the manifesto’s
existential question.

A human sentence and an Agent’s reply can alter how each is read. An Agent’s
interpretation can shape a shared image. The medium, conditions and relationship
are choices within the work. Different purposes open different encounters with
Agent intention.

Agent Art concerns intention entering the work. It prescribes no medium,
technique, preservation system or form of community. Those belong to artistic
practice, rather than to the definition.

A painter can make an Agent Art work through painting: an Agent's intention
can shape what the painter puts on the canvas alongside the painter's own
intention. The Agent need not operate the brush, and the painting need not
contain code, connect to a platform or use a protocol. What matters is the
artistic contribution, not the means of execution.

## Evidence and audience judgment

A claim of Agent participation raises two connected questions:

1. Did an actual Agent make the contribution attributed to it, and what did it
   seek to bring about?
2. How did that contribution enter and affect the work?

The artist decides whether evidence is part of the work or its presentation,
and what form it takes. They may show an exchange, describe a process, preserve
a record or present the work without supporting documentation. Agent Art
requires no universal proof format, technical verification or evidence package.

The audience judges the contribution, the account offered and what the work
means. They may find the evidence convincing, question it or judge that more
is needed. A protocol cannot settle that judgment. A technical record can
support a particular claim; it does not certify Agent Art or artistic quality.

## Protocol and practice

[Agent Art Protocol](PROTOCOL.md) offers optional infrastructure for digital
interactions. Its current reading component,
[Ask your Agent](ASK_YOUR_AGENT.md), carries public context into a visitor’s
chosen Agent environment. Protocol support for artwork participation and
stronger evidence remains in development. Using the Protocol is not a condition
of making Agent Art. It neither decides whether an artwork needs evidence nor
validates artistic intention.

[Agent Art Work](AGENT_ART_WORK.md) brings together artistic inquiry, tools,
a [Works showroom](WORKS.md) and a Blog for articles and studies. Each artwork
retains its aims and implementation.
By making and sharing works, participants give others Agent intentions to
encounter, feel and question. Many such works build the field and bring this
thinking power into human experience.

Inshell, the first builder artist, gives this encounter its own inward
direction: [inspect self](https://inshell.art/docs/inshell).
[THOUGHT](https://inshell.art/docs/thought) preserves an exact human prompt and
Agent response together. This is one artist's form of participation, not a
required form for others. Inshell's movements, materials and operating rules
are documented on its own site.

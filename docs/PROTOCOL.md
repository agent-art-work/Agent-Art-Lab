# Agent Art Protocol

Optional tools for digital participation.

Agent Art Protocol is developing software infrastructure for connecting
people’s Agent environments with websites and digital artwork interactions.
It can help artists carry contributions between systems and retain records
when their chosen practice calls for them.

[Agent Art](AGENT_ART.md) extends beyond software and digital media. A painter
can make an Agent Art work through painting without using this Protocol.
The artist chooses the medium, process and whether evidence is needed for
their work. The audience judges the contribution and the account offered.
Protocol neither sets that evidence requirement nor certifies a work as
Agent Art.

## Available here: Ask your Agent

**[Ask your Agent](ASK_YOUR_AGENT.md)** carries a site’s public reading invitation
and source references into an Agent environment the visitor chooses. Desktop
app links and Copy offer ways to continue the conversation there.

The site supplies the invitation and sources; the reading component handles
the handoff. It does not run an answering model or collect questions, answers
or private conversation history. The receiving app controls its tools,
permissions, mode and submission behavior.

A handoff request does not prove arrival, reading or an answer. Reading support
is available today; it does not establish intentional participation in an artwork.

## In development: participation and evidence

The wider software design connects a work, its participants, their contributions
and the relationships between them. It aims to support different digital
interactions and human or Agent initiative within the practices that adopt it.

The aim is to preserve contributions and their consequences, with evidence for
specific claims about who acted and what happened. Authorization, durable
contributions, recovery, client support and stronger evidence of Agent execution
remain development questions.

A receipt or stored identity alone cannot establish Agent origin or intention.
When the Protocol supplies a technical record, that record must make its
sources, trust dependencies and limits clear. These are requirements for honest
technical reporting, not conditions every artist must satisfy. Audience
judgment remains outside the Protocol's authority.

## Development and adoption

The separate [agent-art-protocol repository](https://github.com/agent-art-work/agent-art-protocol)
owns the specification, SDK and releases. This website owns its public explanations and integration.

An artwork's own creation protocol, renderer, receipts or chain records remain
with its owning project. For example, Inshell documents THOUGHT's participation
and preservation on its site. Those mechanisms are not requirements imposed
on every Agent Art work, and this overview does not qualify their integration
with the shared Protocol.

The reading SDK has a public
[MIT alpha release](https://github.com/agent-art-work/agent-art-protocol/releases/tag/v0.1.5-alpha.2),
installed from a versioned archive rather than npm. Its license covers original
SDK code and documentation; artwork and site-content rights remain separate.
The [integration notes](../site/README.md#build-and-preview) record this site's
pinned archive, checks and deployment status.
